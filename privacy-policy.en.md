## 2. What the app does

La Mouche TV is a **media player**. It plays content supplied by **your own provider** (Xtream Codes-compatible server). The app does not provide, host or sell any audiovisual content.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Data stored only on your device
- Your provider's server address, username and password (the password is encrypted with the Android Keystore).
- Watch history and resume positions, favorites, personal lists, profiles, settings, viewing statistics, parental-control code.

This data is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time: **Settings → Delete my data**, or by uninstalling the app.

### 3.2 Exchanges with your provider
To work, the app sends your credentials to **your server** and retrieves channels, movies, series and program guides. This server is independent from us and its own privacy policy applies. Images (posters, logos) are loaded from addresses supplied by that server; its host may see your IP address.

### 3.3 Optional features
- **Code sync** and **smartphone remote**: exchanges take place only on your local network (Wi-Fi), using a temporary code. Nothing goes through our servers.
- **Google account (cloud sync)**: if you sign in, we process your **email address and Google ID** (via Firebase Authentication) and store in Firebase (Firestore) a **data block encrypted on your device** with your passphrase (favorites, history, lists, profiles, accounts). **We cannot read it.** The sending device's model and the upload date are also recorded. You can delete everything: **Settings → Google account → Delete my cloud data / my account**.
- **Chromecast**: when you cast, the stream address is sent to the chosen Cast device.

### 3.4 Purchases (Premium)
Purchases and subscriptions are processed by **Google Play**. We receive neither your payment details nor your banking data; the app asks Google Play whether a subscription or lifetime purchase is active.

### 3.5 Usage statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)
If you accept (asked at first launch, changeable in **Settings → Statistics and crash reports**), the app enables **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google): Firebase installation ID, device model, Android and app versions, anonymous usage events and technical traces when the app crashes. It **does not use the advertising ID** and **contains no advertising**. Without your consent these tools are disabled.

### 3.6 Problem report (voluntary)
If you use “Report a problem”, your message, the ticked problems, the title of the content concerned, the playback position, the device model and the app version are sent to the developer through **ntfy.sh** and **FormSubmit** (third-party providers). Do not enter sensitive information.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

## 5. Retention
- Local data: until you delete it or uninstall.
- Cloud data: until you delete it (or delete your account).
- Statistics and crashes: period set by Google Firebase (generally 14 months for Analytics and 90 days for Crashlytics).
- Problem reports: as long as needed to handle them.

## 6. Recipients and transfers outside the EU
Cloud data and Firebase tools are provided by **Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC**. Transfers to the United States may occur, covered by GDPR mechanisms (standard contractual clauses, Data Privacy Framework). Problem reports pass through ntfy.sh and FormSubmit. We do not sell or rent any data.
